# About Me

I am **ZeSheng Huang**, a network engineer.

- Data centers, Spine-Leaf, VXLAN EVPN, and ACI
- Firewalls, load balancing, DNS, and network security
- Production network changes, migrations, cutovers, and complex troubleshooting
- Python, monitoring and observability, and network automation
